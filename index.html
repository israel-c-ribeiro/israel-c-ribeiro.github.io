About

Simulation, machine learning and a chemist's intuition.

I am a computational chemist working on the atomic-scale physics and chemistry of functional materials. My Ph.D. at the University of São Paulo focused on the surface passivation of two-dimensional hybrid perovskite thin films, using DFT to resolve how metallic and organic cations change their structure, stability and optoelectronic response.

Today, at UMONS, I build automation and GUI tooling around machine-learning interatomic potentials, run structural searches on hybrid organic–inorganic interfaces and coordinate deliverables across a multi-partner EU consortium of theorists and experimentalists.

Before that I spent three years in industrial analytical labs, and I hold an MBA in Data Science & Analytics. On the side, I founded Quantum Master Inc., a small scientific-consulting venture. What I am looking for now: a research or R&D role where simulation, machine learning and materials chemistry meet.

What I work on

Four threads that run through my Ph.D., my postdoc and the tools I write.

First-principles simulation

Density functional theory of surfaces, interfaces, defects and thin films: structure, energetics, electronic and optical properties, from bulk halide perovskites to 2D Ruddlesden–Popper phases.

ML potentials & structure search

Neural-network interatomic potentials coupled with structural search algorithms to explore hybrid organic–inorganic interfaces, quantify band alignment and process MD trajectories at scale on HPC clusters.

MACENNIPstructure searchMDtight-bindingSlurm

Materials informatics

Clustering, dimensionality reduction and descriptor engineering to screen candidate cations and passivating molecules, shrinking the experimental search space before anything is synthesised.
